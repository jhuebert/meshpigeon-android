# Channels & contacts

## Public channel

The one conversation you start with. Anyone on your region's frequency can
read it — treat it like a town square.

## Starting a channel

**Start chat → Private channel**: a random key nobody can guess. Share it
with a QR or link — only people you invited can read it.

**Start chat → Shared channel** (`#name` style): the key derives from the
name. Anyone who knows the name can join — great for topics, not private.

## Sharing

Every channel has a **Share** action: QR code or a `meshpigeon.app/c/…` link.
Pasting a key is forgiving — spaces and dashes are stripped automatically.
You always see a preview ("Join channel **Trail Talk**?") before joining.

## Contacts

- **Share my contact** pushes your card out so others can find you.
- People whose adverts you hear land in **Contacts → Discovered** — tap to
  add, clear all with a confirmation.
- **Blocking** (long-press a message → "Block @name", or from the contact
  sheet): their messages never render, their adverts are ignored. Reversible
  in Contacts → Blocked.
- Repeaters show under **Radios** — read-only, with a map pin and last
  heard.

## Messages that land well

- Long-press a message to reply, react, copy, mark unread, or delete
  locally (deleting never affects anyone else).
- The composer shows your remaining characters for the current channel —
  messages travel one hop at a time, so brevity wins.
- Delivery states: ⏳ queued → ✓ sent → **✓ heard** (a repeater relayed it)
  → ✓✓ confirmed. If a message fails, long-press → Retry.
