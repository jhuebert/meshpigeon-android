# Getting started with MeshPigeon

*Welcome! This guide assumes you have never heard of mesh radio.*

## What you need

- The MeshPigeon app (Android) — installed from the Play Store.
- A MeshPigeon radio plugged into power (see [radios.md](radios.md)), or one
  you can plug into your phone.

## 1. Install and open the app

No account, no phone number, no internet needed after install.

## 2. Name yourself

One screen: type the name people will see.

## 3. Pick your region

A short list ("Where are you?"). This tunes your radio — that's all it does.
You can change it later in Settings.

## 4. Connect your radio

The app lists nearby radios with friendly names. Tap yours. If you don't
have one yet, tap **Skip for now** — everything else in the app works
offline, and your messages will send when a radio connects.

## 5. Say hello

You land on **Chats** with one conversation: **Public**. Tap the ✏️
**Start chat** button to message a person, or open **Public** and just say
hi.

---

That's the whole required flow. Everything else — channels, contact cards,
the map — is one tap deep, and the advanced stuff (path tracing, identity
backup) is behind Settings → Advanced ([advanced.md](advanced.md)).

## Trouble?

See [troubleshooting.md](troubleshooting.md) — "my message shows ✓ but no
reply", radio not found, and region questions live there.
